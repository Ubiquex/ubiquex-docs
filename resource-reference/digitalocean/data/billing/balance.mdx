## Lookup arguments

_This lookup takes no arguments._

## Result properties

<ResponseField name="account_balance" type="string">
  Current balance of the customer's most recent billing activity. Does not reflect `month_to_date_usage`. Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="generated_at" type="string">
  The time at which balances were most recently generated. Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="month_to_date_balance" type="string">
  Balance as of the `generated_at` time. This value includes the `account_balance` and `month_to_date_usage`. Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="month_to_date_usage" type="string">
  Amount used in the current billing period as of the `generated_at` time. Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>
