`azure_cognitiveservices_cognitiveservices_rai_external_safety_provider_schema` integrates a third-party content-safety guardrail provider (e.g. Palo Alto Networks) into an Azure AI Foundry Responsible AI policy, alongside Microsoft's own built-in filters.
