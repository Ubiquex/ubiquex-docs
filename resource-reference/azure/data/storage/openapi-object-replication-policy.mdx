---
title: "azure_storage_openapi_object_replication_policy"
description: "`data_azure_storage_openapi_object_replication_policy` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_storage_openapi_object_replication_policy`."
---

It configures asynchronous, container-scoped replication of blob(s) from a source storage account to a destination account, for cross-region redundancy or data locality.
