---
title: "azure_netapp_subvolume_info"
description: "`data_azure_netapp_subvolume_info` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_netapp_subvolume_info`."
---

It describes a subvolume, a directory-scoped sub-unit of an Azure NetApp Files volume managed and quota-restricted independently of the rest of the volume.
