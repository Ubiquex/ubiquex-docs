---
title: "azure_resources_openapi_policy_assignment"
description: "`data_azure_resources_openapi_policy_assignment` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_resources_openapi_policy_assignment`."
---

It applies an Azure Policy definition or initiative at a specific scope (a management group, subscription, resource group, or resource), enforcing or auditing its own rule(s) there.
