title: "azure_billing_openapi_product"
description: "`data_azure_billing_openapi_product` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_billing_openapi_product`."

It is a purchased Azure offer (e.g. a reservation or a software plan) billed under a billing account, tracked independently of the subscription it's associated with.
