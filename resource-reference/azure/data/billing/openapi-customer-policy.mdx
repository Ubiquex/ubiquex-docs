---
title: "azure_billing_openapi_customer_policy"
description: "`data_azure_billing_openapi_customer_policy` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_billing_openapi_customer_policy`."
---

It configures policy setting(s) (e.g. whether the customer can view charges) that a CSP partner applies to a customer's own billing account.
