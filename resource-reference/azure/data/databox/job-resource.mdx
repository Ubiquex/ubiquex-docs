---
title: "azure_databox_job_resource"
description: "`data_azure_databox_job_resource` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_databox_job_resource`."
---

It orders a physical Azure Data Box appliance shipped to a customer site for bulk offline data transfer into or out of Azure.
