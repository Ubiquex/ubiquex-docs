---
title: "azure_servicebus_sbnamespace"
description: "`data_azure_servicebus_sbnamespace` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_servicebus_sbnamespace`."
---

It is a Service Bus namespace, the management container that queue(s) and topic(s) are created within.
