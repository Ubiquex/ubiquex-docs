---
title: "azure_cognitiveservices_commitment_plan"
description: "`data_azure_cognitiveservices_commitment_plan` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_cognitiveservices_commitment_plan`."
---

It purchases a fixed monthly commitment tier for an Azure AI services feature (e.g. Translator or Speech), at a discount versus pay-as-you-go pricing.
