---
title: "azure_containerservice_managedclusters_identity_binding"
description: "`data_azure_containerservice_managedclusters_identity_binding` looks up a real, already-existing `azure_containerservice_managedclusters_identity_binding`."
---

It (preview) lets multiple AKS clusters share a single user-assigned managed identity's own federated identity credential for workload identity, working around the per-identity FIC quota that limited large-scale multi-cluster deployments.
