`azure_virtual_machine` is Azure's own core compute resource -- real, on-demand virtualized hardware you configure yourself (size, OS, disks, networking) rather than a managed platform service. It's the choice when a workload needs more control over its own environment than Azure's PaaS options offer, at the cost of still having to patch, configure, and maintain it yourself.
