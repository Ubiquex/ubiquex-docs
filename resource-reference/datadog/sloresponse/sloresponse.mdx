---
title: "datadog_sloresponse"
description: "`datadog_sloresponse` defines a quantified target for how reliably a service should perform over a given time window."
---

SLOs give a team a concrete, shared number to balance new feature work against platform stability, and to communicate reliability honestly with people outside the team who depend on the service.
