`datadog_microsoft_teams_tenant_based_handle_response` is a real, configured Microsoft Teams destination tied to a connected Azure AD tenant -- the newer, tenant-based way Datadog notifications and monitors post into a Teams channel, alongside the older webhook-based handle.
