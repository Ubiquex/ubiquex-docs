`datadog_deployment_gates_evaluation_result_response` is the real, recorded outcome of one `datadog_deployment_gate_response` evaluation -- whether the gate passed or failed for a specific deployment, and the underlying health data that decision was based on.

    <ResponseField name="attributes" type="object" required>

      <Expandable title="properties">
        <ResponseField name="configuration" type="object">

          <Expandable title="properties">
