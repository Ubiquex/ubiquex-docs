`datadog_logs_pipeline` processes incoming logs by running them through an ordered sequence of processors that parse semi-structured text into structured, named attributes. A pipeline only runs against logs matching its own filter, and the standardized attribute names it produces are what let logs from very different sources become searchable, comparable facets without changing anything on the systems producing them.
