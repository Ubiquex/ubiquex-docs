<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha1** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networkconnectivity_hub`.
</Warning>

`google_networkconnectivity_v1alpha1_hub` is the central resource in Network Connectivity Center that VPC network(s), VPN/interconnect connections, and other spoke(s) attach to, enabling any-to-any connectivity across them.
