  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. No stable, generally-available equivalent is published yet.

`google_saasservicemgmt_v1beta1_flag_revision` is a specific, versioned revision of a real flag's own evaluation_spec -- a snapshot of what that flag evaluated to at this revision -- so a flag's own history can be inspected and, if needed, referenced by a real flag_release.
