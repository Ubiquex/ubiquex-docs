`google_saasservicemgmt_rollout` is the real process App Lifecycle Manager uses to update a SaaS offering's provisioned units with a new `google_saasservicemgmt_release`, following the sequencing and pacing strategy defined by its own `google_saasservicemgmt_rollout_kind`.
