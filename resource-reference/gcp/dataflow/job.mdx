<ResponseField name="client_request_id" type="string">
  The client's unique identifier of the job, re-used across retried attempts. If this field is set, the service will ensure its uniqueness. The request to create a job will fail if the service has knowledge of a previously submitted job with the same client's ID and job name. The caller may use this field to ensure idempotence of job creation across retried attempts to create a job. By default, the field is empty and, in that case, the service ignores it. Optional; if omitted, computed by Google Cloud.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="current_state" type="string">
  The current state of the job. Jobs are created in the `JOB_STATE_STOPPED` state unless otherwise specified. A job in the `JOB_STATE_RUNNING` state may asynchronously enter a terminal state. After a job has reached a terminal state, no further state updates may be made. This field might be mutated by the Dataflow service; callers cannot mutate it. Optional; if omitted, computed by Google Cloud.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="current_state_time" type="string">

</ResponseField>
