  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networksecurity_mirroring_endpoint_group`.

`google_networksecurity_v1beta1_mirroring_endpoint_group` is the packet-mirroring entry point associated with a mirroring deployment group, that VPC network traffic copies are routed through.
