Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networksecurity_intercept_endpoint_group`.

`google_networksecurity_v1beta1_intercept_endpoint_group` is the traffic-interception entry point associated with an intercept deployment group, that VPC network traffic is routed through for third-party security appliance inspection.
