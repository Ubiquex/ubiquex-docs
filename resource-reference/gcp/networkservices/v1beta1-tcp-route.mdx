Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networkservices_tcp_route`.

`google_networkservices_v1beta1_tcp_route` defines routing rule(s) for raw TCP traffic within a Cloud Service Mesh, matching on port and directing matching connections to a specific backend.
