<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networkservices_producer_extension`.
</Warning>

`google_networkservices_v1beta1_producer_extension` runs a Service Extension on the producer side of a Private Service Connect-published service, letting the producer inspect or modify traffic before it reaches the underlying service.
