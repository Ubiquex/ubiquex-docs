<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_networkmanagement_connectivity_test`.

`google_networkmanagement_v1beta1_connectivity_test` simulates network traffic between a source and destination to verify whether a connection would actually succeed, tracing through every firewall rule, route, and hop along the way.
