---
title: "google_privateca_ca_pool"
description: "`google_privateca_ca_pool` groups related Certificate Authority Service certificate authorities together..."
---

`google_privateca_ca_pool` groups related Certificate Authority Service certificate authorities together, sharing issuance policy and letting certificates be requested from the pool without pinning to one specific CA.

## Example
