<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_reservation`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_reservation` sets aside real compute capacity -- a specific machine type and count, in a specific zone -- guaranteeing it's actually available for your own VMs to consume, rather than competing for capacity with every other customer at boot time.
