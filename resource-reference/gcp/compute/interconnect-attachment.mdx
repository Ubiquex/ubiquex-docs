`google_compute_interconnect_attachment` attaches a real VLAN to an Interconnect connection, associating that specific slice of the physical link with one of your own VPC networks -- the resource that actually carries your traffic over the interconnect, not the physical connection itself.

Real, typed bindings generated directly from real Google Discovery Documents, in every SDK language -- every tab below is a complete, runnable program, not a fragment, real enough to save and run exactly as shown.
