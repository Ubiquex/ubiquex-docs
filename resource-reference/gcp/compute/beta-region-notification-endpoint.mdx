<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **beta** release channel. Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_region_notification_endpoint`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_beta_region_notification_endpoint` is the regional counterpart to a global notification endpoint -- receiving real status-change notifications from a regional health check service the moment a monitored resource's own health changes.
