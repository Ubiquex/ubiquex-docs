<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_region_instant_snapshot_group`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_region_instant_snapshot_group` is the regional counterpart to a zonal instant snapshot group, capturing a consistent, real snapshot across multiple regional disks at once.
