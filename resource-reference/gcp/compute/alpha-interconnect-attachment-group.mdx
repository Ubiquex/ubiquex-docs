<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_interconnect_attachment_group`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_interconnect_attachment_group` groups multiple real interconnect attachments together so their combined availability and configuration can be managed and monitored as one real unit rather than individually.
