<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **beta** release channel. Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_region_composite_health_check`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_beta_region_composite_health_check` combines the real health states of multiple individual health sources into one overall health verdict for a regional published service -- the service counts as healthy only when enough of its own real health sources agree it is.
