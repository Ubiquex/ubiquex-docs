<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_license`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_license` tracks the real licensing terms and restrictions attached to an image or disk -- the mechanism behind bring-your-own-license and marketplace image billing, not something a user typically creates directly.
