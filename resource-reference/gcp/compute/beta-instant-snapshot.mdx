<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **beta** release channel. Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_instant_snapshot`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_beta_instant_snapshot` is a real, near-instantaneous, zonal point-in-time copy of a persistent disk -- faster to create and restore from than a standard snapshot, at the cost of needing to stay in the same zone as the disk it came from.
