<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_resource_policy`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_resource_policy` is a real, reusable schedule attachable to disks or instances -- automated snapshot creation, or a real start/stop schedule for an instance -- defined once and applied to many resources rather than scripted separately for each.
