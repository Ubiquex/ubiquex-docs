<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_cross_site_network`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_cross_site_network` groups the real Cross-Site Interconnect connections used to link two of your own physical sites directly through Google's network, rather than routing that private, site-to-site traffic over the public internet.
