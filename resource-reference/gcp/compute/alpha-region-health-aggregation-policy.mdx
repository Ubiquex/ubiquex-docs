<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_region_health_aggregation_policy`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_region_health_aggregation_policy` defines the real conditions -- a minimum percentage of healthy backends, a minimum count -- that a composite health check actually applies when deciding whether a regional published service as a whole should be considered healthy.
