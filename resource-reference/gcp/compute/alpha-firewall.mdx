<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_firewall`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_firewall` allows or denies real traffic to and from instances in a VPC network, matched by direction, protocol, port, and source or destination -- the real, network-level access control every instance in the network is subject to.
