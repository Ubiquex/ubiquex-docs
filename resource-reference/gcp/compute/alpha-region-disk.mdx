<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud Compute's own **alpha** release channel -- earlier and less stable than beta. Google may change or remove alpha endpoints at any time, with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_compute_region_disk`.
</Warning>

`google_compute_alpha_region_disk` is a persistent disk replicated synchronously across two zones in a region, rather than living in just one zone -- real protection against a single zone failure, at the cost of the extra real replication overhead.
