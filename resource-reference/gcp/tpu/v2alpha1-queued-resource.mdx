<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v2alpha1** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_tpu_queued_resource`.
</Warning>

`google_tpu_v2alpha1_queued_resource` is a real Cloud TPU queued resource request, letting a workload ask for TPU capacity (optionally against a reservation) and have the actual `google_tpu_node` allocated once that capacity becomes available, rather than failing immediately if it isn't free yet.
