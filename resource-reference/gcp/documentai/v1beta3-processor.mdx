  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta3** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_documentai_processor`.

`google_documentai_v1beta3_processor` is a Document AI processor instance -- a specific document-parsing model (e.g. invoice or form parser) -- that document(s) are sent to for extraction.
