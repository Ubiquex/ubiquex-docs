<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_cloudcontrolspartner_customer`.
</Warning>

`google_cloudcontrolspartner_v1beta_customer` represents a Sovereign Controls by Partners customer relationship, giving a sovereignty partner real, read visibility into that customer's own Assured Workloads (onboarding status, workload metadata) without granting control over the workload itself.
