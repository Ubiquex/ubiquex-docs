<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha1** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_datastream_private_connection`.
</Warning>

`google_datastream_v1alpha1_private_connection` establishes private connectivity (via VPC peering) between Datastream and a source database that isn't reachable over the public internet.
