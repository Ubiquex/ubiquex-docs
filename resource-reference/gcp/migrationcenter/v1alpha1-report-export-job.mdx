<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha1** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. No stable, generally-available equivalent is published yet.
</Warning>

`google_migrationcenter_v1alpha1_report_export_job` is a real, tracked export of a Migration Center report to an external destination (signed_uri_destination), recording its own recent_executions, so a report export can be scheduled and audited as its own resource rather than a one-off, untracked download.
