<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha1** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_migrationcenter_assets_export_job`.
</Warning>

`google_migrationcenter_v1alpha1_assets_export_job` exports discovered asset inventory data from Migration Center to an external destination (e.g. BigQuery), for reporting or integration outside the console.
