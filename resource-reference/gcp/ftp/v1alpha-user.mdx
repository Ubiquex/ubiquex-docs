<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_ftp_user`.
</Warning>

`google_ftp_v1alpha_user` is a real user account provisioned on a `google_ftp_server`, the credential and access-control unit that authorizes SFTP clients to connect and transfer files into or out of the backing Cloud Storage bucket.
