<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_aiplatform_persistent_resource`.
</Warning>

`google_aiplatform_v1beta1_persistent_resource` is a long-running, pre-provisioned compute cluster on Vertex AI that multiple training jobs or pipeline runs can reuse, avoiding the provisioning delay of spinning up fresh compute for each one.
