<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_aiplatform_tuning_job`.
</Warning>

`google_aiplatform_v1beta1_tuning_job` fine-tunes a foundation model (e.g. a Gemini model) on Vertex AI using caller-supplied training data, producing a new, specialized tuned model version.
