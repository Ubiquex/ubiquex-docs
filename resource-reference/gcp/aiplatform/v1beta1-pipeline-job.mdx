Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_aiplatform_pipeline_job`.

`google_aiplatform_v1beta1_pipeline_job` runs a Vertex AI Pipelines (Kubeflow Pipelines-based) workflow -- an ordered graph of ML step(s) such as data prep, training, and evaluation -- as a single orchestrated execution.
