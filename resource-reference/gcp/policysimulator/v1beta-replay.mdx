<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_policysimulator_replay`.
</Warning>

`google_policysimulator_v1beta_replay` is a Policy Simulator replay, re-evaluating real past IAM access attempts under both a resource's current policy and a proposed policy change, then comparing the two results to show exactly how a member's access would change if the proposed policy were applied.
