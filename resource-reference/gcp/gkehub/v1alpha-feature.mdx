<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_gkehub_feature`.
</Warning>

`google_gkehub_v1alpha_feature` enables a specific fleet-wide capability (e.g. Config Sync, Multi-cluster Ingress, or the Identity Service) across GKE clusters registered with a fleet via GKE Hub.
