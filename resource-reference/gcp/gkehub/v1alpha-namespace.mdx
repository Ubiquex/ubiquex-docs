  This is Google Cloud's own **v1alpha** release channel (alpha). Google may change or remove alpha/preview endpoints at any time, often with no deprecation notice -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. `google_gkehub_v1alpha_namespace` is a real fleet-scoped namespace -- its own namespace_labels and the scope it belongs to -- letting a namespace name and its labels be managed consistently across every cluster in a fleet scope rather than configured cluster by cluster.
