<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta1** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_netapp_quota_rule`.
</Warning>

`google_netapp_v1beta1_quota_rule` sets a per-user or per-group storage quota on a Google Cloud NetApp Volumes volume, limiting how much space or how many files that user or group may consume.
