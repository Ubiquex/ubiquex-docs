<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v2beta2** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_cloudtasks_task`.

`google_cloudtasks_v2beta2_task` is a single unit of deferred or scheduled work enqueued in a Cloud Tasks queue, dispatched to its own target when its own schedule time arrives.
