<Warning>
  This is Google Cloud's own **v1beta2** release channel (beta). Google may change or remove beta endpoints as a feature moves toward general availability -- confirm this endpoint is still live before depending on it in anything long-lived. The stable, generally-available equivalent is `google_memcache_instance`.
</Warning>
