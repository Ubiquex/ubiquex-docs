`aws_ses_mail_manager_ingress_point` creates a real, authenticated inbound email endpoint, SMTP or PrivateLink, that applies an `aws_ses_mail_manager_traffic_policy` and `aws_ses_mail_manager_rule_set` to every message it receives before deciding whether to accept, block, or route it.
