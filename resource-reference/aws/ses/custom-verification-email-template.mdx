`aws_ses_custom_verification_email_template` defines a real, branded email template used when SES asks a new sender identity to verify ownership of their email address, replacing SES's own generic default verification email.

## Properties

<ResponseField name="failure_redirection_url" type="string" required>
  The URL that the recipient of the verification email is sent to if his or her address is not successfully verified. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="from_email_address" type="string" required>
  The email address that the custom verification email is sent from. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="success_redirection_url" type="string" required>
  The URL that the recipient of the verification email is sent to if his or her address is successfully verified. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tags" type="list(object)">
  The tags (keys and values) associated with the tenant. Optional.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="key" type="string">
      The key part of a tag attached to the Amazon SES custom verification email template, used to categorize or identify the template. Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS. **(AI-inferred)**
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="value" type="string">
      Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="template_content" type="string" required>
  The content of the custom verification email. The total size of the email must be less than 10 MB. The message body may contain HTML, with some limitations. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="template_name" type="string" required>
  The name of the custom verification email template. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="template_subject" type="string" required>
  The subject line of the custom verification email. Required.
</ResponseField>
