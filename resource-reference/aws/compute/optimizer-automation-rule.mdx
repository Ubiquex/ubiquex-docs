---
title: "aws_compute_optimizer_automation_rule"
description: "`aws_compute_optimizer_automation_rule` automatically applies EBS volume optimization recommendations..."
---

`aws_compute_optimizer_automation_rule` automatically applies EBS volume optimization recommendations, cleaning up unattached volumes and upgrading to newer volume types, on a real recurring schedule against resources matching a Region or tag filter, with a dashboard tracking every automated action and estimated savings, and the ability to reverse one.
