`aws_batch_quota_share` allocates a real portion of an AWS Batch service quota, such as vCPU capacity, to a specific `aws_batch_compute_environment` or account, letting quota be divided predictably across teams or workloads rather than competed for on a first-come basis.

## Input properties

<ResponseField name="capacity_limits" type="list(object)" required>
  A list that specifies the quantity and type of compute capacity allocated to the quota share. Required.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="capacity_unit" type="string">
      Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="max_capacity" type="number">
      Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="job_queue" type="string" required>
  The AWS Batch job queue associated with the quota share. This can be the job queue name or ARN. A job queue must be in the `VALID` state before you can associate it with a quota share. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="preemption_configuration" type="object" required>
  Required.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="in_share_preemption" type="string" required>
      Specifies whether jobs within a quota share can be preempted by another, higher priority job in the same quota share. Required.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="quota_share_name" type="string" required>
  The name of the quota share. It can be up to 128 characters long. It can contain uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens (-), and underscores (_). Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="resource_sharing_configuration" type="object" required>
  Required.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="borrow_limit" type="number">
      The maximum percentage of additional capacity that the quota share can borrow from other shares. `BorrowLimit` can only be applied to quota shares with a strategy of `LEND_AND_BORROW`. This value is expressed as a percentage of the quota share's configured CapacityLimits. The `BorrowLimit` is applied uniformly across all capacity units. For example, if the `BorrowLimit` is 200, the quota share can borrow up to 200% of its configured `maxCapacity` for each capacity unit. The default `BorrowLimit` is -1, which indicates unlimited borrowing. Optional.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="strategy" type="string" required>
      The resource sharing strategy for the quota share. The `RESERVE` strategy allows a quota share to reserve idle capacity for itself. `LEND` configures the share to lend its idle capacity to another share in need of capacity. The `LEND_AND_BORROW` strategy configures the share to borrow idle capacity from an underutilized share, as well as lend to another share. Required.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="state" type="string">
  The state of the quota share. If the quota share is `ENABLED`, it is able to accept jobs. If the quota share is `DISABLED`, new jobs won't be accepted but jobs already submitted can finish. The default state is `ENABLED`. Optional.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tags" type="any">
  The tags that you apply to the quota share to help you categorize and organize your resources. Each tag consists of a key and an optional value. Optional.
</ResponseField>

## Output properties

<ResponseField name="quota_share_arn" type="string">
  Computed by AWS after `ship`.
</ResponseField>
