`aws_access_analyzer_analyzer` creates an IAM Access Analyzer, a real analyzer that continuously scans resource policies (S3 buckets, IAM roles, KMS keys, and similar) and reports which grant access to anything outside the account or organization it's scoped to. Analyzers come in a few real, distinct modes, external access, unused access, and internal access, and the same resource also drives policy validation and policy generation from real CloudTrail activity.

## Input properties

Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS. **(AI-inferred)**

You can choose a value between 1 and 365 days. Optional.
