---
title: "aws_sesv2_email_identity_policies"
description: "`data_aws_sesv2_email_identity_policies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ses_email_identity`."
---

It verifies a real sending domain or individual email address with SES, the identity-verification step required before SES will send mail from it, and the resource DKIM and custom MAIL FROM settings are configured against.
