---
title: "aws_sesv2_custom_verification_email_templates"
description: "`data_aws_sesv2_custom_verification_email_templates` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ses_custom_verification_email_template`."
---

It defines a real, branded email template used when SES asks a new sender identity to verify ownership of their email address, replacing SES's own generic default verification email.
