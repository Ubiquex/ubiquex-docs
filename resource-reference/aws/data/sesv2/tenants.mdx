---
title: "aws_sesv2_tenants"
description: "`data_aws_sesv2_tenants` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ses_tenant`."
---

It creates a real, isolated tenant within an SES account, giving a SaaS platform sending on behalf of many customers its own separated sending identities, reputation, and metrics per tenant rather than one shared pool for the whole account.
