---
title: "aws_eks_addon_versions"
description: "`data_aws_eks_addon_versions` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_eks_addon`."
---

It installs and manages a real, EKS-curated operational component, the VPC CNI, CoreDNS, kube-proxy among common ones, on an `aws_eks_cluster`, with its own version tracking and update lifecycle handled by EKS rather than a caller applying manifests and managing upgrades by hand.
