---
title: "aws_config_conformance_pack_compliance_details"
description: "`data_aws_config_conformance_pack_compliance_details` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_config_conformance_pack`."
---

It bundles a real collection of `aws_config_config_rule`s and remediation actions into one deployable, monitorable unit, so an entire compliance standard's worth of rules gets rolled out and tracked together instead of one rule at a time.
