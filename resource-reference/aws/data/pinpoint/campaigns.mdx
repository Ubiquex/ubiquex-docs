---
title: "aws_pinpoint_campaigns"
description: "`data_aws_pinpoint_campaigns` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_pinpoint_campaign`."
---

It creates a real, scheduled or triggered messaging campaign targeting an `aws_pinpoint_app` segment. Campaigns are explicitly named among the engagement features AWS is retiring: the Pinpoint console and campaign resources become inaccessible after October 30, 2026, a date roughly two months from now, with AWS recommending Amazon Connect for continued engagement use cases.
