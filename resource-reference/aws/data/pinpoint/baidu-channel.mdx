---
title: "aws_pinpoint_baidu_channel"
description: "`data_aws_pinpoint_baidu_channel` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_pinpoint_baidu_channel`."
---

It enables a real push-notification channel on an `aws_pinpoint_app` for Android devices in China reachable through Baidu Cloud Push, subject to the same October 30, 2026 Pinpoint retirement as Pinpoint's other channel resources.
