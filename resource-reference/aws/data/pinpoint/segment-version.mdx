---
title: "aws_pinpoint_segment_version"
description: "`data_aws_pinpoint_segment_version` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_pinpoint_segment`."
---

It defines a real, filtered subset of an `aws_pinpoint_app`'s endpoints, by attribute, behavior, or location, that an `aws_pinpoint_campaign` targets. Segments are explicitly named among the engagement features retiring when the Pinpoint console and resources become inaccessible after October 30, 2026, roughly two months from now.

<ResponseField name="application_id" type="string" required>
  The real, unique identifier of the application this lookup is scoped to. **(AI-inferred)** Required lookup argument.
</ResponseField>

  The real, unique identifier of the segment this lookup is scoped to. **(AI-inferred)** Required lookup argument.
</ResponseField>

  The real version identifier for this object. **(AI-inferred)** Required lookup argument.
</ResponseField>
