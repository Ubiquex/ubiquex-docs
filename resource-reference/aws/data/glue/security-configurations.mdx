---
title: "aws_glue_security_configurations"
description: "`data_aws_glue_security_configurations` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_glue_security_configuration`."
---

It sets real encryption settings, at rest and in transit, applied to an `aws_glue_job`, `aws_glue_crawler`, or development endpoint when it runs, kept as its own reusable resource rather than repeated per job.
