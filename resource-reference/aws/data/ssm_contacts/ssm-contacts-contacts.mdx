---
title: "aws_ssm_contacts_contacts"
description: "`data_aws_ssm_contacts_contacts` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ssmcontacts_contact`."
---

It creates a real, on-call contact, an individual or an escalation plan, in Incident Manager Contacts, that an `aws_ssmincidents_response_plan` engages when an incident is declared.
