---
title: "aws_organizations_resource_policy"
description: "`data_aws_organizations_resource_policy` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_organizations_resource_policy`."
---

It attaches a real resource-based policy at the AWS Organizations management account level, a narrower, newer counterpart to the identity- and OU-targeted `aws_organizations_policy`, controlling which principals can call Organizations APIs against the organization itself.
