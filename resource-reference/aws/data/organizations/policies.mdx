---
title: "aws_organizations_policies"
description: "`data_aws_organizations_policies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_organizations_policy`."
---

It creates a real policy, a service control policy, tag policy, backup policy, AI services opt-out policy, or declarative policy, that can be attached to an AWS Organization, an `aws_organizations_organizational_unit`, or an `aws_organizations_account` to govern what that target and everything under it can do.
