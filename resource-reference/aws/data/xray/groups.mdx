---
title: "aws_xray_groups"
description: "`data_aws_xray_groups` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_xray_group`."
---

It defines a real, named filter expression over X-Ray trace data, so traces matching specific criteria, an error rate threshold or a particular service, can be viewed and alarmed on as their own group rather than sifted out of the account's full trace volume by hand.
