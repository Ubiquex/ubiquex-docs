---
title: "aws_connect_traffic_distribution_group_users"
description: "`data_aws_connect_traffic_distribution_group_users` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_connect_traffic_distribution_group`."
---

It links `aws_connect_instance`s in different AWS Regions and distributes real inbound traffic to phone numbers attached to the group between them, the mechanism behind multi-Region resiliency and disaster recovery for a contact center.
