---
title: "aws_connect_instances"
description: "`data_aws_connect_instances` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_connect_instance`."
---

It provisions the real, top-level container for a contact center, telephony, chat, and every other Connect resource, agents, queues, contact flows, attaches to it. As of this session, AWS now uses "Amazon Connect" as the name of a broader portfolio of agentic business-function solutions, and refers to the contact center product this resource belongs to as "Amazon Connect Customer" (or simply Customer) in its own documentation, the two names used interchangeably during the transition.
