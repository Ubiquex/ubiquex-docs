---
title: "aws_rds_dbproxies"
description: "`data_aws_rds_dbproxies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_rds_dbproxy`."
---

It creates a real, fully managed connection pooler in front of one or more `aws_rds_dbinstance`s or an `aws_rds_dbcluster`, absorbing bursts of application connections without exhausting the database's own native connection limit.
