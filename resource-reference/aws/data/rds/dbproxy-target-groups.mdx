---
title: "aws_rds_dbproxy_target_groups"
description: "`data_aws_rds_dbproxy_target_groups` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_rds_dbproxy_target_group`."
---

It configures a real connection-pooling and failover behavior for the specific `aws_rds_dbcluster` or instances an `aws_rds_dbproxy` routes to.
