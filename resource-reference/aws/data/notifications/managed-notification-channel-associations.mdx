`data_aws_notifications_managed_notification_channel_associations` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_notifications_managed_notification_additional_channel_association`. It adds a real, additional delivery channel beyond an account's default contacts to an AWS-managed notification configuration, extending who receives one of AWS's own pre-built notifications.
