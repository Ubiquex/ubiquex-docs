---
title: "aws_imagebuilder_image_pipelines"
description: "`data_aws_imagebuilder_image_pipelines` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_image_builder_image_pipeline`."
---

It automates the recurring build, test, and distribution of a real machine image or container image, combining an `aws_image_builder_image_recipe` or `aws_image_builder_container_recipe` with build infrastructure and an `aws_image_builder_distribution_configuration` into one scheduled, repeatable process.
