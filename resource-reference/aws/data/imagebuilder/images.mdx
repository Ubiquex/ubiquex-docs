---
title: "aws_imagebuilder_images"
description: "`data_aws_imagebuilder_images` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_image_builder_image`."
---

It is the real, actual output, an AMI or container image, produced by running an `aws_image_builder_image_pipeline` once, either on its own recurring schedule or triggered manually.
