title: "aws_ssm_ops_summary"
description: "`data_aws_ssm_ops_summary` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ssm_ops_item`."

It creates a real, trackable operational issue, an incident, change request, or general to-do, in OpsCenter, with its own status, priority, and related resources, for centralizing operational work instead of tracking it in a separate ticketing system.
