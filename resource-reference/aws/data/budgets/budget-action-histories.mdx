title: "aws_budgets_budget_action_histories"
description: "`data_aws_budgets_budget_action_histories` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_budgets_budgets_action`."

It attaches a real, automatic response, applying an IAM policy that denies further provisioning, for instance, to an `aws_budgets_budget` crossing its threshold, turning a budget from a passive alert into an enforced spending control.
