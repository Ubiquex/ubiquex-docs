---
title: "aws_budgets_budgets"
description: "`data_aws_budgets_budgets` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_budgets_budget`."
---

It tracks real AWS cost, usage, Reserved Instance utilization/coverage, or Savings Plans utilization/coverage against a defined threshold, fixed or growing, and can notify by email or SNS when actual or forecasted spend crosses it.
