---
title: "aws_lookoutequipment_inference_schedulers"
description: "`data_aws_lookoutequipment_inference_schedulers` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_lookout_equipment_inference_scheduler`."
---

It schedules real, recurring inference runs against a trained Amazon Lookout for Equipment model, feeding it fresh sensor data on a cadence and surfacing predicted anomalies. Lookout for Equipment closed to new customers on October 7, 2025, and AWS has stated it will be fully discontinued on October 7, 2026, a date roughly six weeks from now; existing customers can keep using it as normal until then, with AWS continuing security, availability, and performance investment but no new features.
