---
title: "aws_logs_delivery_sources"
description: "`data_aws_logs_delivery_sources` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_logs_delivery_source`."
---

It represents a real AWS resource, such as a VPC or a Route 53 resolver, registered as a source of vended logs through the newer CloudWatch Logs delivery framework, the origin end of an `aws_logs_delivery`.
