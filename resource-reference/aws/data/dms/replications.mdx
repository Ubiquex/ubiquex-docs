---
title: "aws_dms_replications"
description: "`data_aws_dms_replications` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_dms_replication_config`."
---

It defines a real, serverless replication, DMS automatically provisions, scales, and manages the underlying compute rather than a caller sizing and managing an `aws_dms_replication_instance` by hand.
