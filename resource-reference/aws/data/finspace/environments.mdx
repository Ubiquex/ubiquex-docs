---
title: "aws_finspace_environments"
description: "`data_aws_finspace_environments` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_fin_space_environment`."
---

It provisions a real Amazon FinSpace environment, a managed data catalog and analytics workspace, notebook-based, kdb Insights, or dataset-browser, purpose-built for financial industry data. Amazon FinSpace closed to new customers on October 7, 2025, with end of support scheduled for October 7, 2026; existing customers can continue using it in the interim.
