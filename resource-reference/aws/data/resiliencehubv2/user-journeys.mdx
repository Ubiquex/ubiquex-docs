---
title: "aws_resiliencehubv2_user_journeys"
description: "`data_aws_resiliencehubv2_user_journeys` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_resilience_hub_v2_user_journey`."
---

It describes a real, critical business path, the sequence of services and steps a user actually depends on, through an `aws_resilience_hub_v2_system`, letting resilience be assessed against outcomes that matter to users rather than only individual services in isolation.
