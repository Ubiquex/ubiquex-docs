---
title: "aws_resiliencehubv2_policies"
description: "`data_aws_resiliencehubv2_policies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_resilience_hub_v2_policy`."
---

It defines a real, modular, composable resiliency policy under the next-generation AWS Resilience Hub model, reusable and assignable across multiple `aws_resilience_hub_v2_service`s at once rather than one application at a time. This next-generation model, with generative-AI-powered failure mode analysis and organization-wide reporting, launched in May 2026, roughly three months ago, so behavior here is verified live rather than assumed from general knowledge.
