It represents a real business application under the next-generation AWS Resilience Hub model (relaunched May 2026), the top-level resource that `aws_resilience_hub_v2_service`s and `aws_resilience_hub_v2_user_journey`s are organized under and that an `aws_resilience_hub_v2_policy` is ultimately assessed against.
