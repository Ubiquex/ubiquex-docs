---
title: "aws_dlm_lifecycle_policies"
description: "`data_aws_dlm_lifecycle_policies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_dlm_lifecycle_policy`."

It automates the real, recurring creation, retention, and deletion of EBS snapshots and EBS-backed AMIs on a defined schedule, so backups happen consistently without a caller manually creating or cleaning up snapshots.
