## Lookup arguments

<ResponseField name="key_id" type="string" required>
  The real, unique identifier of the encryption key this lookup is scoped to. **(AI-inferred)** Required lookup argument.
</ResponseField>

## Result properties

<ResponseField name="key_rotation_enabled" type="bool">
  Whether the key rotation is currently enabled. **(AI-inferred)** Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="next_rotation_date" type="string">
  The real date of the next rotation. **(AI-inferred)** Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="on_demand_rotation_start_date" type="string">
  The real date of the on demand rotation start. **(AI-inferred)** Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="rotation_period_in_days" type="number">
  The real period this secret's own rotation runs on, in days. **(AI-inferred)** Returned by the lookup.
</ResponseField>
