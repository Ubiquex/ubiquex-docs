---
title: "aws_greengrass_logger_definitions"
description: "`data_aws_greengrass_logger_definitions` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_greengrass_logger_definition`."
---

It configures real, local logging, destination and log level, for an `aws_greengrass_group`'s core device and its Lambda functions. Part of Greengrass V1's maintenance-only lifecycle since June 2023.
