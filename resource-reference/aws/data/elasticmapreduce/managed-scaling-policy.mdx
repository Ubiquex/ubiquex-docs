  &lt;p&gt; Managed scaling policy for an Amazon EMR cluster. The policy specifies the limits for resources that can be added or terminated from a cluster. The policy only applies to the core and task nodes. The master node cannot be scaled after initial configuration. &lt;/p&gt; Returned by the lookup.
