---
title: "aws_observabilityadmin_telemetry_rules_for_organization"
description: "`data_aws_observabilityadmin_telemetry_rules_for_organization` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_observability_admin_organization_telemetry_rule`."
---

It applies a real `aws_observability_admin_telemetry_rule`-style telemetry configuration across every account in an AWS Organization at once, rather than one account at a time.
