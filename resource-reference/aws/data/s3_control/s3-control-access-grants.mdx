---
title: "aws_s3_control_access_grants"
description: "`data_aws_s3_control_access_grants` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_s3_access_grant`."
---

It grants a real IAM principal or an IAM Identity Center user or group temporary, credential-vended access to a specific S3 prefix through an `aws_s3_access_grants_instance`, without writing or maintaining a bucket policy for that grant.
