---
title: "aws_invoicing_invoice_units"
description: "`data_aws_invoicing_invoice_units` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_invoicing_invoice_unit`."
---

It groups a real, mutually exclusive set of member accounts into one business entity, a subsidiary, a legal entity, a cost center, so AWS issues that entity its own separate invoice rather than one combined invoice for an entire AWS Organization. Reached general availability December 2024.
