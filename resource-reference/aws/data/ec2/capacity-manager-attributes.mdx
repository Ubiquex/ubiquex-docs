---
title: "aws_ec2_capacity_manager_attributes"
description: "`data_aws_ec2_capacity_manager_attributes` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ec2_capacity_manager_data_export`."
---

It configures a real, scheduled export of EC2 Capacity Manager's own usage data, On-Demand, Spot, and Capacity Reservation metrics across an organization's accounts and Regions, to an S3 bucket. Amazon EC2 Capacity Manager reached general availability October 17, 2025, roughly ten months ago.
