---
title: "aws_ec2_client_vpn_authorization_rules"
description: "`data_aws_ec2_client_vpn_authorization_rules` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ec2_client_vpn_authorization_rule`."
---

It grants a real, specific group of connected users access to a defined network CIDR through an `aws_ec2_client_vpn_endpoint`, without it, a connected client reaches nothing regardless of network association.
