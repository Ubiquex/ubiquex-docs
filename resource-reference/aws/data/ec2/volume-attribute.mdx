---
title: "aws_ec2_volume_attribute"
description: "`data_aws_ec2_volume_attribute` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ec2_volume`."
---

It creates a real, persistent Amazon EBS block storage volume, attachable to an EC2 instance, that keeps its own data independently of any single instance's own lifecycle.
