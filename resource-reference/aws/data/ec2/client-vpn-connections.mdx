---
title: "aws_ec2_client_vpn_connections"
description: "`data_aws_ec2_client_vpn_connections` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ec2_client_vpn_endpoint`."
---

It provisions a real, managed OpenVPN-based endpoint that individual users connect to over a client VPN application, terminating their connection and routing them into an associated VPC, distinct from `aws_customer_gateway`'s site-to-site model connecting whole networks together.
