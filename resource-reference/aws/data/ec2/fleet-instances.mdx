---
title: "aws_ec2_fleet_instances"
description: "`data_aws_ec2_fleet_instances` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ec2_fleet`."
---

It launches a real, defined mix of On-Demand, Spot, and Reserved Instance capacity across multiple instance types and Availability Zones from one request, rather than a caller managing separate launch requests per purchasing option.
