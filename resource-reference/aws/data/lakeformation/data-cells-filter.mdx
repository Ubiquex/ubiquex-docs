---
title: "aws_lakeformation_data_cells_filter"
description: "`data_aws_lakeformation_data_cells_filter` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_lake_formation_data_cells_filter`."
---

It defines a real, fine-grained filter, restricting access to specific rows or columns of a table, that Lake Formation enforces on top of its own coarser table- and database-level `aws_lake_formation_permissions` grants.
