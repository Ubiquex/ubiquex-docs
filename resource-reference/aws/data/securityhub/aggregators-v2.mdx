---
title: "aws_securityhub_aggregators_v2"
description: "`data_aws_securityhub_aggregators_v2` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_security_hub_aggregator_v2`."
---

It configures real cross-Region and cross-account aggregation of findings and risk data under Security Hub's next-generation (HubV2) model, the V2 counterpart to the original `aws_security_hub_finding_aggregator`. Security Hub's major relaunch, unifying findings across GuardDuty, Inspector, Macie, and Security Hub CSPM with near-real-time risk analytics, was announced at re:Invent in December 2025, roughly eight months ago.
