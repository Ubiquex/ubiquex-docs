---
title: "aws_pinpoint_email_dedicated_ips"
description: "`data_aws_pinpoint_email_dedicated_ips` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_pinpoint_email_dedicated_ip_pool`."
---

It groups real dedicated sending IP addresses under an `aws_pinpoint_email_configuration_set`, so a chosen subset of an account's dedicated IPs is used for a particular category of email, subject to the same October 30, 2026 Pinpoint retirement.
