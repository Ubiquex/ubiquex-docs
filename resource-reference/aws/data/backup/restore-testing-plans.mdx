---
title: "aws_backup_restore_testing_plans"
description: "`data_aws_backup_restore_testing_plans` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_backup_restore_testing_plan`."
---

It schedules AWS Backup to automatically restore a sample of real recovery points on a recurring basis and validate the restore succeeded, catching a broken or unrestorable backup before an actual disaster does, rather than trusting an untested backup plan.
