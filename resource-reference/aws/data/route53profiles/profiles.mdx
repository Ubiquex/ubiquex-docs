---
title: "aws_route53profiles_profiles"
description: "`data_aws_route53profiles_profiles` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_route53_profiles_profile`."
---

It creates a real Route 53 Profile, a reusable bundle of DNS configuration, private hosted zone associations, Resolver rules, DNS Firewall rules, that can be applied consistently across many VPCs at once rather than configured VPC by VPC.
