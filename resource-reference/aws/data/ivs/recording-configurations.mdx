---
title: "aws_ivs_recording_configurations"
description: "`data_aws_ivs_recording_configurations` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ivs_recording_configuration`."
---

It automatically records an `aws_ivs_channel`'s live stream to a real S3 bucket as it broadcasts, so a stream is available for on-demand playback after it ends without a caller building their own recording pipeline.
