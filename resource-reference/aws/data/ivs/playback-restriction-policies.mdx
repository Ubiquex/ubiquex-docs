---
title: "aws_ivs_playback_restriction_policies"
description: "`data_aws_ivs_playback_restriction_policies` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_ivs_playback_restriction_policy`."
---

It restricts real playback of an `aws_ivs_channel` by geography or origin domain, blocking viewers outside an allowed country list or a site that isn't the caller's own from embedding the stream.
