---
title: "aws_lightsail_container_log"
description: "`data_aws_lightsail_container_log` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_lightsail_container`."
---

It creates a real Lightsail Container Service, a managed environment that deploys and runs containerized applications, images pushed via Docker, without the operational surface of a full ECS or EKS cluster.
