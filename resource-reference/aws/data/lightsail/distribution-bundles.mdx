title: "aws_lightsail_distribution_bundles"
description: "`data_aws_lightsail_distribution_bundles` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_lightsail_distribution`."

It creates a real content delivery network distribution, powered by CloudFront under the hood, that caches and accelerates delivery of content hosted on a Lightsail instance, bucket, or load balancer.
