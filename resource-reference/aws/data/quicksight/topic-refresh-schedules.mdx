---
title: "aws_quicksight_topic_refresh_schedules"
description: "`data_aws_quicksight_topic_refresh_schedules` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_quick_sight_refresh_schedule`."
---

It schedules real, recurring refreshes of an `aws_quick_sight_data_set`'s underlying data, on a daily, weekly, monthly, or custom cadence.
