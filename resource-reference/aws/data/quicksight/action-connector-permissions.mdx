---
title: "aws_quicksight_action_connector_permissions"
description: "`data_aws_quicksight_action_connector_permissions` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_quick_sight_action_connector`."
---

It connects a real external system, a SaaS application or internal API, to Amazon Quick (the AI-workspace product that Amazon QuickSight was renamed into, first as Amazon Quick Suite on October 9, 2025 and shortened to Amazon Quick in 2026), letting its agents take real write actions in that system rather than only read data from it. The underlying CloudFormation namespace remains `AWS::QuickSight::*` despite the product rename.
