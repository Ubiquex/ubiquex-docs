---
title: "aws_quicksight_account_custom_permission"
description: "`data_aws_quicksight_account_custom_permission` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_quick_sight_custom_permissions`."
---

It defines a real, named set of restricted capabilities, disabling the ability to share dashboards, for example, that can be assigned to specific QuickSight/Amazon Quick users or groups, layered on top of standard Author or Reader permissions.
