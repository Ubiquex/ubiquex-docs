---
title: "aws_frauddetector_labels"
description: "`data_aws_frauddetector_labels` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_fraud_detector_label`."
---

It tagged real historical events as fraudulent or legitimate for training an `aws_fraud_detector_event_type`'s own model. Closed to new customers along with the rest of Fraud Detector since November 7, 2025.
