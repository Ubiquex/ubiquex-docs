---
title: "aws_shield_resources_in_protection_group"
description: "`data_aws_shield_resources_in_protection_group` looks up a real, already-existing `aws_shield_protection_group`."
---

It groups real `aws_shield_protection`-covered resources together so Shield Advanced evaluates and reports on their combined traffic pattern as one unit, useful when resources share load or are meant to be assessed together rather than individually.

    func main() {
    	ubx.Main(ubx.Stack("example", func() {
