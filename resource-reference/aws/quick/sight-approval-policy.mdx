`aws_quick_sight_approval_policy` defines a real rule governing when an `aws_quick_sight_action_connector`'s write actions require human approval before they execute versus running automatically, part of Amazon Quick's own agentic-action governance.

## Input properties

<ResponseField name="actions" type="list(any)" required>
  List of governed actions a policy applies to. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="applicable_to" type="object" required>
  Scoping: who the policy applies to. GROUP: `groupArns` required (one or more group ARNs). Required.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="group_arns" type="list(string)">
      Required when type = GROUP. One or more group ARNs. Optional.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="any" required>
      Required.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="approval_groups" type="list(string)" required>
  List of approval group ARNs (e.g. QuickSight group ARNs). At least one approval group is required; the upper bound is enforced per-account at the service layer via the configurable approver-group limit. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="asset_types" type="list(any)" required>
  List of asset types a policy applies to. At least one asset type is required. Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="description" type="string">
  Optional.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="name" type="string" required>
  Required.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="policy_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier for a QuickSight approval policy that governs whether asset publishing requires approval before the asset is shared. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>
