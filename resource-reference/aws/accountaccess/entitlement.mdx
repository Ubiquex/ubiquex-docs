`aws_account_access_entitlement` is the entitlement half of IAM Identity Center's account access manager, the specific permission grant attached to an `aws_account_access_application`. It is what actually authorizes a principal to assume the application's target role, real, revocable access rather than an implicit side effect of the application existing.

    	}))
    }
