---
title: "aws_pinpoint_email_channel"
description: "`aws_pinpoint_email_channel` enables a real email-sending channel on an `aws_pinpoint_app`, backed by Amazon SES."
---

Like Pinpoint's other channels, this becomes inaccessible after October 30, 2026 (roughly two months from now), but ongoing email sending capability continues directly under Amazon SES, which AWS recommends as the migration target for Pinpoint email workloads.

## Input properties

<ResponseField name="application_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier for the Amazon Pinpoint application (project) that the email channel is associated with. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="configuration_set" type="string">
  The name of the Amazon SES configuration set that is applied to emails sent through this Amazon Pinpoint email channel. Optional. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="enabled" type="bool">
  Specifies whether the email channel is enabled for the Amazon Pinpoint application. Optional. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="from_address" type="string" required>
  The verified email address that Amazon Pinpoint uses as the sender for emails sent through this channel. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="identity" type="string" required>
  Specifies the Amazon SES identity (a verified email address or domain) that Amazon Pinpoint uses to send email through this channel. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="orchestration_sending_role_arn" type="string">
  The ARN of the IAM role that Amazon Pinpoint assumes to submit email through Amazon SES on behalf of your account for orchestrated sends, such as messages sent via journeys or campaigns on this email channel. Optional. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="role_arn" type="string">
  The ARN of the IAM role that Amazon Pinpoint assumes to send email through Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) on behalf of the application's email channel. Optional. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

## Output properties

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  The `id` field stores the ApplicationId of the Amazon Pinpoint application associated with this email channel, as returned by the Pinpoint API. Computed by AWS after `ship`. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>
