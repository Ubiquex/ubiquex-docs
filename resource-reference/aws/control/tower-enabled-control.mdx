`aws_control_tower_enabled_control` turns on a real, high-level governance rule, preventive, detective, or proactive, against an organizational unit or account in an `aws_control_tower_landing_zone`, the actual mechanism that keeps an environment from drifting away from its intended security and compliance baseline.
