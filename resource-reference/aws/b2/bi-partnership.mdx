`aws_b2_bi_partnership` represents the actual trading relationship with a business partner, tying together an `aws_b2_bi_profile` and one or more `aws_b2_bi_capability` resources into the configuration AWS B2B Data Interchange uses to exchange documents with that specific partner correctly.
