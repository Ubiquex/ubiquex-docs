title: "aws_customer_profiles_segment_definition"

---

`aws_customer_profiles_segment_definition` groups real customer profiles sharing defined attributes, including calculated ones, into a segment that's dynamically re-evaluated as underlying attribute values change, rather than a static, one-time export of matching profiles.
