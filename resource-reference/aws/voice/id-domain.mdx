`aws_voice_id_domain` created a real domain for Amazon Connect Voice ID, enrolling and matching caller voiceprints for real-time fraud detection during a call. Amazon Connect Voice ID is now fully discontinued: it closed to new customers on May 20, 2025, and AWS ended support entirely on May 20, 2026, a date roughly three months in the past. This resource describes a service no longer operable rather than a current one.
