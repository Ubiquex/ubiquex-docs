`aws_shield_proactive_engagement` configures real, designated emergency contacts and enables the DRT to reach out proactively when Shield Advanced detects an in-progress DDoS event, rather than waiting for the customer to open a support case first.

## Input properties

<ResponseField name="emergency_contact_list" type="list(object)" required>
  A list of email addresses and phone numbers that the Shield Response Team (SRT) can use to contact you for escalations to the SRT and to initiate proactive customer support. To enable proactive engagement, the contact list must include at least one phone number. Required.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="contact_notes" type="string">
      A free-form note field attached to an emergency contact, used to record additional context such as the contact's role or best time to reach them. Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS. **(AI-inferred)**
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="email_address" type="string">
      The email address where AWS Shield Advanced sends proactive engagement notifications and alerts for this emergency contact. Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS. **(AI-inferred)**
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="phone_number" type="string">
      The phone number of the emergency contact for AWS Shield Advanced proactive engagement notifications. Optional; if omitted, computed by AWS. **(AI-inferred)**
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="proactive_engagement_status" type="string" required>
  If `ENABLED`, the Shield Response Team (SRT) will use email and phone to notify contacts about escalations to the SRT and to initiate proactive customer support. If `DISABLED`, the SRT will not proactively notify contacts about escalations or to initiate proactive customer support. Required.
</ResponseField>

## Output properties

<ResponseField name="account_id" type="string">
  The AWS account ID that the Shield Advanced proactive engagement configuration applies to; this is automatically resolved to the current account and is read-only. Computed by AWS after `ship`. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>
