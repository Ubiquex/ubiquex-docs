`aws_connect_campaigns_v2_campaign` is Outbound Campaigns V2, the real, actively developed successor to `aws_connect_campaigns_campaign` with multi-channel (voice, SMS, email, WhatsApp) and visual journey-builder support that V1 doesn't have.
