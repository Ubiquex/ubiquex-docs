`aws_ec2_vpcencryption_control` monitors, or in enforce mode actually blocks, cleartext traffic within and across VPCs in a Region, real, centralized enforcement of encryption in transit. T3, T3a, and T4g instances don't support the encryption VPC Encryption Controls requires and can't be used in an enforced subnet. AWS began charging for this feature by the hour per enabled VPC starting March 1, 2026, after a free introductory period.
