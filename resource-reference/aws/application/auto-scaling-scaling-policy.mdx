`aws_application_auto_scaling_scaling_policy` defines how an `aws_application_auto_scaling_scalable_target` actually scales, target tracking against a CloudWatch metric, step scaling, scheduled scaling, or predictive scaling, the real logic a registered target has none of until a policy is attached.
