`aws_ground_station_dataflow_endpoint_group` registers the real network endpoints, an AWS Ground Station Agent endpoint or traditional dataflow endpoints, that satellite data actually flows to or from during a contact.

## Input properties

<ResponseField name="contact_post_pass_duration_seconds" type="number">
  Amount of time, in seconds, after a contact ends that the Ground Station Dataflow Endpoint Group will be in a POSTPASS state. A Ground Station Dataflow Endpoint Group State Change event will be emitted when the Dataflow Endpoint Group enters and exits the POSTPASS state. Optional.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="contact_pre_pass_duration_seconds" type="number">
  Amount of time, in seconds, before a contact starts that the Ground Station Dataflow Endpoint Group will be in a PREPASS state. A Ground Station Dataflow Endpoint Group State Change event will be emitted when the Dataflow Endpoint Group enters and exits the PREPASS state. Optional.
</ResponseField>
