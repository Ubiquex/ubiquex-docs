`aws_fin_space_environment` provisions a real Amazon FinSpace environment, a managed data catalog and analytics workspace, notebook-based, kdb Insights, or dataset-browser, purpose-built for financial industry data. Amazon FinSpace closed to new customers on October 7, 2025, with end of support scheduled for October 7, 2026; existing customers can continue using it in the interim.
