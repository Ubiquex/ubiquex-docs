`aws_workspaces_pool` creates a real pool of non-persistent, session-based virtual desktops that are provisioned on demand and torn down after use, WorkSpaces' pooled-desktop model for shared or task-worker use cases, distinct from a `aws_work_spaces_workspace`'s own persistent, one-user-per-desktop model.
