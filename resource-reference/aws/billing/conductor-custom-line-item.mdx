`aws_billing_conductor_custom_line_item` adds a manually defined charge or credit, a flat fee, a support markup, a negotiated discount, into an `aws_billing_conductor_billing_group`'s pro forma cost data, alongside its real, pricing-plan-derived AWS costs.
