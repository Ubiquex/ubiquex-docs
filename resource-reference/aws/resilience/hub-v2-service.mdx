`aws_resilience_hub_v2_service` registers a real deployable unit, its resources discovered from a CloudFormation stack, Terraform state, or an EKS cluster, under the next-generation AWS Resilience Hub model (launched May 2026), the counterpart to the original model's `aws_resilience_hub_app`, assessed against an `aws_resilience_hub_v2_policy`.
