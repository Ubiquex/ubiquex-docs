`aws_sim_space_weaver_simulation` ran a real, large-scale spatial simulation, distributing simulated entities like vehicles or pedestrians across a managed compute cluster. AWS SimSpace Weaver is now fully discontinued: it closed to new customers on May 20, 2025, and AWS ended support entirely on May 20, 2026, a date roughly three months in the past. This resource describes a service no longer operable rather than a current one.
