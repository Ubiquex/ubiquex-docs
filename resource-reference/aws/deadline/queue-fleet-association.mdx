---
title: "aws_deadline_queue_fleet_association"
description: "`aws_deadline_queue_fleet_association` is the real, actual link between an `aws_deadline_queue` and an `aws_deadline_fleet`."
---

A queue with no fleet association has nowhere to actually schedule its jobs to run.

## Properties

<ResponseField name="farm_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the AWS Deadline Cloud farm that contains the queue and fleet being associated. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="fleet_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the fleet that is associated with the queue in AWS Deadline Cloud. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="queue_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the Deadline queue to associate with the fleet, linking the queue to the specified fleet. Required. **(AI-inferred)**
</ResponseField>
