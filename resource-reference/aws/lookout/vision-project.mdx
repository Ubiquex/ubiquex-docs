---
title: "aws_lookout_vision_project"
description: "`aws_lookout_vision_project` was a real project container for training and hosting Amazon Lookout for Vision computer-vision defect-detection models."
---

Lookout for Vision is now fully discontinued: it closed to new customers on October 10, 2024, and shut down entirely on October 31, 2025, a date now in the past, so this resource describes a service no longer operable rather than a current one.
