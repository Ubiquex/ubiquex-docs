`aws_acmpca_certificate_authority` creates a private certificate authority, root or subordinate, that issues and revokes X.509 certificates for internal PKI. It's regionally scoped, integrates with services like ACM and IoT Core for automated certificate issuance, and its private key never leaves AWS's managed HSM boundary.
