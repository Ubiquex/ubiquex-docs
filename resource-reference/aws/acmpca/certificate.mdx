---
title: "aws_acmpca_certificate"
description: "`aws_acmpca_certificate` is a certificate issued by an `aws_acmpca_certificate_authority`."
---

AWS Private CA issues it against the CA's own signing key and policy, the certificate itself carries no independent configuration beyond what its issuing CA allows.
