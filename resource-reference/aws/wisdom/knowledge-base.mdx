`aws_wisdom_knowledge_base` creates a real content source, synced from S3, a website, or a supported connector, that an `aws_wisdom_assistant`'s AI agents retrieve information from to ground their generated answers.

</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="name" type="string" required>
