---
title: "ubx stats"
description: "Decision-flow metrics, computed entirely from the ledger itself."
---

Real numbers about how your team actually uses signed proposals, never
asserted from outside, computed by folding your own ledger. What
fraction of drift gets resolved through the real, reviewed flow instead
of quietly ignored, how long decisions actually take, how much of your
drift carries real attribution.

## Usage

```bash
ubx stats [flags]
```

## Examples

```bash
ubx stats
```

```bash
ubx stats: 94 proposal(s) total

By kind:
  adoption: 12
  change: 68
  drift_adopt: 10
  drift_revert: 4

Acceptance method:
  local: 90 (96%)
  pr_merge: 4 (4%)

Signed-flow drift resolution: 100% (14 of 14 surfaced drift(s) resolved -- 10 adopted/superseded, 4 reverted, 0 still open)
  (measures drift THIS LEDGER has a record of -- a drift never scanned,
  or scanned and never accepted, leaves no trace for an offline fold to find)

Attribution coverage: 80% (8 of 10 drift_adopt proposal(s) carry a real actor)

Mean time-to-decision: 4m12s (14 sample(s))
```

## Flags

<ResponseField name="--ledger-dir" type="string" default=".">
  Root directory containing `ledger/` and `.ubx/` (default `.`).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="--stack" type="string">
  Restrict the report to one stack. Required for a remote ledger store.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="--since / --until" type="string">
  RFC3339 timestamps, restricting the window of proposals counted.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="--json">
  Emit one JSON document instead of human text.
</ResponseField>

## Notes

Every metric is named honestly for what it can and can't measure. Drift
resolution only ever counts drift *this ledger has a record of*, a drift
that was never scanned in the first place leaves nothing for an offline
fold to find. This report never claims more certainty than the ledger
itself actually contains.
