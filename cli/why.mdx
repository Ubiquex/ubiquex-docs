---
title: "ubx why"
description: "Explain an accepted proposal, or a resource's full proposal history"
---

```text
ubx why <proposal-id> [flags]
ubx why <stack>.<type>.<name> [flags]
```

Explains either one accepted [proposal](/concepts/proposal) (by its full hash),
or a resource's entire recorded history (by its `<stack>.<type>.<name>`
address) — newest first. Can also re-verify a PR-merge acceptance against
current git history and the GitHub API with `--verify-acceptance`.

<Note>
  Full flag reference and verified examples land in a follow-up documentation
  session. In the meantime, `ubx why --help` is authoritative.
</Note>
