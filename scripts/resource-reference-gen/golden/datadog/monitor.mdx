---
title: "datadog_monitor"
description: "`datadog_monitor` watches a metric, an integration's own availability, or a network endpoint against a real threshold, and alerts when it's crossed."
---

Monitors are Datadog's own core detection mechanism -- routing notifications to the right people with the context needed to act, so problems get caught before they affect customers rather than after.
