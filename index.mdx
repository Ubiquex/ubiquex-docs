---
title: "Ubiquex"
description: "AI-native infrastructure change management via a signed proposal ledger."
---

Ubiquex is an AI-native infrastructure change management tool built around
a signed proposal ledger instead of a mutable state file. Every change,
however it's authored (by hand, by AI, or generated from a diagram),
becomes a reviewed, signed proposal before it ever touches real
infrastructure. It stays as a permanent, verifiable record of who decided
what, and why.
