---
title: "Why"
description: "Explaining an accepted proposal, or a resource's entire history"
---

`ubx why` answers "what happened, and why" for anything in the
[ledger](/concepts/ledger). It takes either of two forms of argument:

- **A proposal ID** — the full 64-character hash. Explains that one proposal:
  its intent, its delta's attribution (if any), who accepted it and how, and its
  blast radius.
- **A resource address** (`<stack>.<type>.<name>`) — explains that resource's
  entire recorded history: its adoption, plus every subsequent drift, newest
  first.

## One proposal

```bash
ubx why 89f98a80c66dc1eab122080eb33166980e9b69b0b119a141b978a270aa6ddea1 --ledger-dir .
```

```text
proposal 89f98a80c66dc1eab122080eb33166980e9b69b0b119a141b978a270aa6ddea1 (change)
stack:  payments
status: accepted
intent: provision the payments database
  source: manual_edit PR #42 (content_hash=)
accepted by [roozbeh] via local at 2026-07-11T15:42:26Z
blast radius: +1 ~0 -0
```

## A resource's whole history

```bash
ubx why payments.fake_widget.api-cache --ledger-dir .
```

```text
payments.fake_widget.api-cache: 2 proposal(s), newest first
- drift_adopt 944279d49fe5… (2026-07-11T15:42:52Z): record drift on payments.fake_widget.api-cache observed outside the ledger
- adoption daaa09786cd6… (2026-07-11T15:42:41Z): adopt existing payments.fake_widget.api-cache into the ledger (discovered by scan)
```

Proposal IDs in this view are truncated for readability — that's a display
choice only, never used to look anything up. Pass the full ID (shown in `ubx
accept`'s output, or in the raw ledger file) to `ubx why` directly for the full,
single-proposal view.

## Re-checking a PR-merge acceptance

A proposal accepted via `ubx accept --from-merge` can be independently
re-verified at any time — not just trusted from when it was first accepted —
with `ubx why <id> --verify-acceptance`. This re-checks that the merge commit
still exists and the proposal file still hashes correctly, and, given
`--github-repo`, re-fetches the pull request's current reviewers to check
whether anything has changed since. See the [CLI reference for `ubx
why`](/cli/why) for the full flag set, and [`ubx accept`](/cli/accept) for how
PR-merge acceptance itself works.
