---
title: "Drift, Adoption & Revert"
description: "The honest response when reality and the ledger disagree."
---

The ledger only knows what it was explicitly told. If someone changes a
real resource by hand, outside ubx entirely, the ledger has no idea
until something goes looking for the difference. That gap, between what
the ledger believes and what's actually true right now, is called
drift.

## How it works

`ubx status` and `ubx scan` read a resource's real, live state and
compare it, attribute by attribute, against what the ledger last
recorded for it. Any mismatch is drift, and it's never silently ignored.

```bash
ubx scan --propose both
```

```bash
! payments.aws_sqs_queue.pipeline-events drifted
  message_retention_seconds: 86400 -> 1209600
  next: ubx ship <adopt-hash>   (accept the new value)
       ubx ship <revert-hash>  (restore the signed value)
```

Once drift is found, there are exactly two honest responses, drafted
side by side:

- **Adopt**: accept the real value as the new truth. This only updates
  the ledger's own memory, nothing on the actual infrastructure changes.
- **Revert**: undo the drift, restoring the infrastructure to match what
  was originally signed off on.

## Why silently "fixing" drift automatically doesn't work

A tool that automatically reverted anything unexpected, without asking,
would fight real, intentional changes that just happened to be made
outside it, an emergency fix at 2am, say. A tool that automatically
adopted everything would let unreviewed changes quietly become the new
accepted baseline with nobody ever actually deciding that was okay.
Neither guess is safe to make on your behalf.

## What this actually looks like

Where a real audit backend like CloudTrail is available, `ubx why`
names who actually made the change, not just that something changed.
See [Attribution](/concepts/attribution) for the full mechanism and a
real, worked example.

## Why it matters

Drift is the honest name for "reality and the ledger disagree." Naming
it plainly, and requiring an explicit human decision either way, means
nothing about your real infrastructure's history is ever quietly
assumed or guessed at.
